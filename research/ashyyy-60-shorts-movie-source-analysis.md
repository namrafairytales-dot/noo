## 2. Chori ka Formula — yeh log movie ko short kaise banate hain

Saari 12 seedhi copies mein yahi pattern baar baar dikhta hai:

1. **Saare naam hata do.** Simba → "I", Mufasa → "my dad", Scar → "my uncle", Nala → "a girl from my kingdom",
   Timon & Pumbaa → "two mates". Kisi bhi copy mein ek bhi character ka asli naam nahi hai. Isse search karne par
   movie nahi milti aur viewer ko lagta hai "real story" hai.
2. **Animal / cartoon ko insaan bana do.** Lion King mein sher → insaan ("boot", "hands bleeding"),
   wildebeest → "a herd". TMNT mein "ooze" → "canister of chemicals".
3. **First person "I" mein bolo.** Movie third-person dikhaati hai; short "main ne dekha" bana deta hai, jaise
   kisi ki apni aap-beeti ho. 60 mein se 57 shorts first person mein hain.
4. **POV side-character ka le lo.** Charlie ki jagah **Willy Wonka** bolta hai (02), Light ki jagah uska **police
   chief father** (19), Brian ki jagah **Dom jaisa crew leader** (18), hero ki jagah **villain Megamind** (11).
   Is se story "nayi" lagti hai jabke plot same hota hai.
5. **Hook = movie ka sabse bara dard ya premise, ek line mein.** Movie ka sabse famous scene pehli line mein:
   - Lion King → *"My dad died in front of me when I was six. And for the next 12 years I believed I killed him."*
   - A Quiet Place → *"My son dropped a spoon and I almost lost my family."*
   - Avatar → *"I ran away from home for one night, and when I came back, my entire village was empty."*
6. **Numbers daalo taake "real" lage.** "12 years", "$340,000", "456 people", "64 of them", "$3,000",
   "15 years", "200 men". Kuch numbers movie wale hi hain (456, 64, $3,000, four years old), kuch badal diye (218, 12).
7. **Movie ko order-wise compress karo.** Hook ke baad story wapas shuru se chalti hai aur movie ke beats
   bilkul usi tarteeb mein aate hain, har beat ek-do sentence.
8. **Climax kabhi nahi dikhate — midpoint par cliffhanger.** Lion King Nala ke aane par khatam (Scar se fight
   nahi), Squid Game game 5 par khatam, Fast & Furious heist se pehle, Spider-Man warehouse par, Death Note cameras
   par, Megamind final fight se pehle. Viewer ko "Part 2?" wali bhook lagti hai.
9. **Famous dialogue ko paraphrase karo, copy nahi.**
   - *"With great power comes great responsibility"* → *"Power without responsibility is just a disaster with better aim."*
   - *"Run away, Simba. Run away and never return."* → *"Run and never come back."*
10. **Aakhri line apni ghar ki bana do** taake twist "original" lage: Squid Game ka recruiter raat ko kamre mein aata
    hai, Fast & Furious mein *"Let's see whose family you actually chose."*, Death Note mein *"He never looked at one of them"*.
11. **Title se source chhupa do.** Lion King = "Stolen Throne", Squid Game = "Final Game", Avatar = "Frozen
    Survivor", TMNT aur Megamind dono = "Hidden Heroes". Titles ek hi template se bane hain: *Hidden / Final / Silent /
    Stolen + noun + emoji*.

se fight nahi dikhaai.

| Movie | Short |

| Mufasa | "my dad" / "the king" |
| Scar | "my uncle" |

| Scar ke claws | "his boot" |
| Timon & Pumbaa | "two mates" |
| Jungle | "a forest" |
| Nala | "A girl from my kingdom" |

**Line-by-line match:**

| Short ki exact line | Movie ka scene |

| "My dad fell back past me and he did not get up again." | Mufasa girta hai |

Movie mein Simba ne Scar ko dhakka dete **nahi** dekha tha, isi liye guilt ka sense banta tha. Short ne yeh tod diya.

### Poore channel ke hook patterns
1. **"Impossible contradiction"** — pehli line mein aisi baat jo ho hi nahi sakti:
   "...to thank us for my dad's heart" (dad zinda hai), "My son is 23" (school attendance), "handing us things we have not
