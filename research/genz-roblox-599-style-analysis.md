## 4. How the jokes work

- **Roast comeback:** take their words literally and flip them. "It's an elevator, not your living room."
- **Deadpan literal:** "Bro, I don't have a right hand. This is as high as it gets."
- **Absurd chain of 3:** "The piano played its last song. The elephant needed therapy."
- **Gamer words:** final boss, NPC, bot lobby, uninstall, lag.
- **Stock lines:** "I froze" (39 times in the top 100), "That's when" (25), "Her face went red as a tomato".
- **Side-character mic-drop:** a kid, little brother or friend says the last line.
- **Twist:** a hidden truth flips the story (the lifeguard paid the kid, the cop was a kid in his dad's uniform).

## 5. Engagement

- Titles: Xenost uses "She said the elevator is hers!" + 💀/☠️. JimStoriez uses misspelled caps + 🥀 + #satire.
- Series with a recurring premise (courtroom pt 1-3, brother superpowers) score millions on every part.
- An end question is rare (2 of the top 100) but works when it is a joke: "How do I go broke?"

## 6. Watch out

Many hits use dark jokes (funerals, guns, slaps). For our channel: keep the roast and the twist, drop the
deaths and weapons (see Model 3, section 7).
