# I yelled at a kid for breaking my phone… biggest REGRET of my life 💔 (original, regret → real help)

Regret in line 1. Open loop in the middle (why is this kid doing every job in town?). Twist: the jar label.
Ending: the narrator helps the right way, and the kid's bell hints that he knows. No violence.

```
I yelled at a 9-year-old for breaking my phone, and it's the biggest regret of my life.
He was riding his bike way too fast and crashed right into me.
My phone hit the ground. Screen shattered.
His bike broke too. The chain snapped.
I screamed, "Do you know how much this costs?!"
He just whispered, "I'm sorry," and dragged his broken bike away.
The next day, I saw him washing cars in the rain.
The day after, he was selling lemonade outside my building.
Then he was walking 4 dogs at once. Well, the dogs were walking him.
I thought, "This kid is weird."
A week later, someone knocked on my door.
It was him. Holding a big jar full of coins.
"$86 and 40 cents. It's all I have. Is it enough for your phone?"
I didn't know what to say.
Then I saw the label on the jar.
It said "BIKE FUND." But "BIKE" was crossed out.
Under it, in crayon: "HIS PHONE."
He'd been saving for a new bike for 2 years.
And he gave every coin to the guy who yelled at him.
I took the jar and said, "Deal."
That night, me and my friends added every dollar we had.
The next morning, a brand-new bike was waiting at his door.
With a note: "Phone's fixed. Keep riding. And slow down a little."
He never found out it was me.
But every day he rides past my window... and rings his bell twice.
```
