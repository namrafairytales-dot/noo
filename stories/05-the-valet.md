# The Valet

```
A rich guy threw his car keys at my face
Three hours later, his house was empty
I park cars at the biggest wedding hall in town
He pulled up in a brand-new black SUV
"One scratch and I'll get you fired"
No tip
His friends laughed
I parked his car at the far end of the lot
And went back to the gate
I didn't leave that gate all night
At midnight, I brought his car around
He didn't even look at me
The next morning, police came to the hall
His house was robbed during the wedding
Gold. Cash. Watches
The lock wasn't broken
Someone had used a key
And only one person had his keys that night
Me
They checked the cameras
I never left the gate
They let me go

His car keys? His house key was on the same ring
His address? I pressed "Home" on his GPS
At the far end of the lot, my cousin was waiting
I slid the house key off and handed it to him
Two hours later, he brought me a coffee
The key was under the cup
He was worried about one scratch on his car
And handed me the key to his house
```
