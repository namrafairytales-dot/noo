# He said "go play with your grandma"… so I BROUGHT my grandma 💀 (original revenge, teen-simple)

Simple words, one place (a basketball court), a clear bully, a funny revenge, and a last line that calls back
to the first one. No violence.

```
A big kid kicked me off the basketball court and said, "This court is mine. Go play with your grandma."
"Bro, it's a public court."
"Exactly. I'm the public."
"You're not the public. You're the problem."
His friends laughed. Then he threw my ball over the fence.
"Come back when you can dunk, shorty."
So I went home and told my grandma.
My grandma is 75, walks with a stick, and yells at basketball on TV every single night.
She put down her tea and said, "Tomorrow. 4 o'clock."
The next day, I walked onto the court with my grandma.
The kid fell on the floor laughing. "Bro really brought his grandma."
Grandma said, "First to 3. If you lose, this court isn't yours anymore."
He said, "Granny, I don't want to break your hip."
She just took off her slippers.
First shot, from half court. Swish.
He blinked.
Second shot, she spun past him so fast he fell on his butt.
His ankles filed a complaint.
Third shot, my 75-year-old grandma jumped... and dunked.
The whole park went silent.
Then his coach came running over and froze.
"Coach? You... you trained me when I was 10."
Grandma said, "And you still didn't teach this kid any manners?"
The coach turned red as a tomato. "Apologize. And 50 push-ups. Now."
The kid did push-ups while Grandma sipped her tea on the bench.
Then she grabbed her stick, looked down at him, and said, "Now go play with YOUR grandma."
```
