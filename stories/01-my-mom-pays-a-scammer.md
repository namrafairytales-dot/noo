# My Mom Pays A Scammer Every Month

Length: ~176 spoken words, about 40 seconds at normal storytime pace.

```
My mom pays a scammer $300 a month.
And she knows it's a scam.
Same guy. Same terrible fake accent. Two years straight.
"Ma'am, your bank account has been hacked."
And she just… pays him.
So last month, I grabbed the phone to go off on him.
But before I could say a word, he coughed.
That little cough my brother does every time he lies.
My brother hasn't talked to Mom in 3 years.
Not since the fight.
I looked at Mom. She wasn't even surprised.
She took the phone back and said, "Yes sir, I'll send it right now."
After he hung up, she said, "Don't you dare tell him."
"It's the only time I get to hear his voice."
So I checked her bank app. She never does.
She hasn't lost a single dollar.
The morning after every call, $300 comes back.
Sender: my brother.
They're both pretending…
just to hear each other.
And I'm never telling either of them.
Because the moment one of them finds out…
the calls stop.
```

## Voiceover pauses
- Short pause after "he coughed."
- Short pause after "Sender: my brother."
- Longest pause before "the calls stop."
