# My Mom Pays A Scammer Every Month

Crisp line-by-line style. About 150 spoken words, roughly 40 seconds.

## English

```

And she knows
Same guy
Same fake accent
"Ma'am, your bank account's been hacked"
And she pays
Every single time
Last month, I grabbed the phone
Before I could say a word
He coughed
I'd know that cough anywhere
My brother does it every time he lies
The same brother who hasn't talked to Mom in 3 years
I looked at Mom
Zero shock
She took the phone back
"Yes sir, sending it now"
He hung up
She said, "Don't you dare tell him"
"It's the only time he calls"
So I opened her bank app
She never does
Every month, $300 goes out
And the next morning, $300 comes back
Sender: my brother
Two years of scam calls
And nobody's lost a dollar
So I'm keeping my mouth shut
Because the day one of them finds out…
the calls stop
```
