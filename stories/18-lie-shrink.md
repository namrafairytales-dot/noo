# In this world, every LIE makes you shrink 1 inch 💀 (original "In this world" story, teen-simple)

Rule in line 1. Hidden clue in line 2 ("Good thing I never lie" is itself a lie). Three funny examples, each with
its own rule of 3. Then the narrator gets caught by his own rule. Ends with a comment question. No violence.

```
In this world, every time you lie, you shrink 1 inch.
Good thing I never lie.
Monday, the school bully told the teacher, "I didn't take his lunch money."
Shrink.
"I swear I didn't."
Shrink.
"I'm literally the nicest kid in this school."
Shrink, shrink, shrink.
By lunch, he was shorter than the kid he bullied.
The kid patted him on the head and took his lunch money back.
Tuesday, my sister brought her boyfriend home to meet my dad.
"Sir, I have a job."
Shrink.
"And a car."
Shrink.
"And I love your cooking."
He disappeared inside his own shoes.
My dad looked at my sister and said, "I like this one. He's easy to carry."
Wednesday, our teacher said, "This test will be super easy."
She shrank so much she had to climb her own desk like a mountain.
That's how we knew the test was going to be impossible.
But the worst day was Thursday.
My mom stood in the kitchen holding an empty cookie jar.
"Who ate the last cookie?"
Everyone looked at me.
I said, "Not me."
Shrink.
"I was at my friend's house."
Shrink.
"I don't even like cookies."
I got so small I fell right into the cookie jar.
Covered in crumbs.
My mom looked down at me and said, "Found him."
Remember when I said I never lie?
Yeah... I'm telling you this story from inside a cookie jar.
What lie would make YOU the smallest?
```
