# The Pawn Shop Guitar

```
A pawn shop owner called my grandpa's guitar junk
Ten minutes later, he paid me $10K for it
He offered $100 first
I said no
Then a man in a suit walked in
He saw the guitar and stopped
"Is that a 1959 original?"
"I'll pay $50K. Cash"
"Give me one hour to get the money"
He left his card on the counter and walked out
The owner leaned in
"Guys like that never come back"
"I'll give you $10K. Right now"
I said, "You said it was junk"
He said, "I made a mistake"
I took the cash
And left him the guitar

The man in the suit? My cousin
The card? A dead number
My grandpa? Never touched a guitar in his life
That owner told me two things that day
"It's junk"
And "Guys like that never come back"
He was right… twice
```
