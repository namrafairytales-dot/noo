# The Pawn Shop Guitar (v2: stronger hook, longer)

```
A pawn shop owner offered me $100 for my grandpa's guitar
Ten minutes later, he begged me to take $10K
He laughed the second he saw it
"It's junk. Your grandpa got scammed"
I was halfway to the door
When a man in a suit walked in
He saw the guitar and stopped
He flipped it over and checked the number inside
His hands were shaking
"Is that a 1959 original?"
"I'll pay $50K. Cash"
"Give me one hour to get the money"
"Don't sell it to anyone"
He left his card on the counter and walked out
The owner leaned in
"Guys like that never come back"
"I'll give you $5K. Right now"
I said no
"$8K"
I said, "He's paying 50"
"$10K. Cash. Final offer"
I looked at the door
Then at the money
And took the cash

The man in the suit? My cousin
The card? A dead number
The number inside the guitar? I wrote it myself
My grandpa? Never touched a guitar in his life
That owner told me two things that day
"It's junk"
And "Guys like that never come back"
He was right… twice
```
