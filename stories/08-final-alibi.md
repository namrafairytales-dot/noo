# Final Alibi 🎬 (from the movie Drishyam, 2015)

```
My daughter killed the police chief's son, and all I had to save her was every movie I'd ever watched.
I wasn't even home.
I'm an orphan who left school after 4th grade.
My family is all I have.
At a camp, the boy had secretly filmed my daughter.
One night he came to our house to blackmail her.
She swung a metal rod at his phone.
It hit his head instead.
My wife and daughter buried him in our backyard.
My youngest watched them do it.
Across town, they were building a new police station.
I sent his phone off in the wrong direction.
I made his car disappear.
We took a family trip, and I kept every receipt.
Every shopkeeper there remembered our faces.
They just remembered the wrong day.
We rehearsed one story until it was perfect.
The police beat us for the truth.
Nobody changed a word.
Then they hit my youngest.
She told them the spot.
They dug.
They found a dead calf.
The boy's mother quit her job.

Now I sign in at the new police station.
The boy? He's under the floor.
Every day, a whole police station walks right over him.
```

## Source map (not for the voiceover)

- Movie: Drishyam (Hindi, 2015), a remake of the Malayalam Drishyam (2013). Told from the father's side.
- Every beat follows the film in order: the camp video, the blackmail, the rod, the backyard burial that the
  younger girl watches, the phone sent on a false trail, the car gone, the trip with receipts, the witnesses
  remembering the wrong day, the police beating, the younger girl giving up the spot, the calf in the pit, the
  boy's mother resigning, and the father signing in at the new police station.
- The body under the police station: the first film's last scene shows this only by hint. Drishyam 2 confirms it.
- Changed: all names and places removed (no Goa, no Panaji). The police station line is moved earlier as a
  hidden clue for viewers to catch on a rewatch.
- Mine (not in the film): the hook's wording and the last line.
