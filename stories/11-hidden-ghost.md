# Hidden Ghost 👻 (from the movie Interstellar, 2014)

```
When I was a kid, a ghost kept knocking books off my shelf
My dad didn't believe me
Then it drew lines in the dust on my floor
My dad cracked the code
Map coordinates
They led us to a secret space base
They asked him to fly into space and find humans a new planet
He gave me a watch and promised he'd come back
I didn't even say goodbye
Out there, 1 hour on one planet cost 7 years of mine
On one birthday, I turned the same age he was when he left
Still no dad

Then one day, I went back to my old room
The watch he gave me was ticking weird
Morse code
The answer that saved everyone on Earth
The ghost? My dad
Talking to me from inside a black hole
When I saw him again, I was an old lady
He looked exactly the same
Every dad says he'll be back
Mine came back through my bookshelf
```

Source: told by the daughter (Murph). The ghost, the dust coordinates, the watch, 1 hour = 7 years, the birthday
message, the Morse code on the watch and the black hole are all in the film. The last 2 lines are mine.
