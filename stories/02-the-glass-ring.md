# The Glass Ring

```
A woman stole a $50K ring right in front of me
Thirty seconds later, it was in my hand
I caught her at the corner
Grabbed her wrist
She didn't fight back
She just opened her fist
I took the ring
And held it up so the whole street could see
The owner ran over, out of breath
He kissed the ring
And put it straight back in his window
He didn't even press charges
"I got my ring back. That's enough"
He tried to hand me $5K
I said, "Keep it, sir"
Everyone clapped

The woman? My sister
The ring I held up? Glass
The real one? Up my sleeve since I grabbed her wrist
Why not just run?
Because cops hunt a stolen ring
Nobody hunts a ring that came back
And the $5K?
Why take 5… when you already have 50
That glass ring is still in his window
Priced at $50K
```

## Roman Urdu: Kaanch Ki Ring

```
Meri aankhon ke saamne ek aurat 50 lakh ki ring le bhaagi
Tees second baad, woh ring mere haath mein thi
Maine usay mor pe pakad liya
Kalaai pakdi
Woh lari bhi nahi
Bas muthi khol di
Maine ring li
Aur upar kar ke poori gali ko dikhai
Dukaan ka maalik haanpta hua aaya
Ring ko chooma
Aur seedha wapas showcase mein rakh di
Police case bhi nahi kiya
"Ring mil gayi. Bas kaafi hai"
Mujhe 5 lakh inaam dene laga
Maine kaha, "Rehne dein, sir"
Sab ne taaliyan bajayin

Woh aurat? Meri behen
Jo ring maine dikhai? Kaanch ki
Asli wali? Kalaai pakadte hi meri aasteen mein
Seedha bhaag kyun nahi gaye?
Kyunki chori hui ring ko police dhoondti hai
Wapas aayi ring ko koi nahi dhoondta
Aur woh 5 lakh?
5 kyun loon… jab 50 jeb mein hain
Woh kaanch ki ring aaj bhi uske showcase mein hai
Price tag: 50 lakh
```
