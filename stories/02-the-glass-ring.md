# The Glass Ring (v2: stronger hook, longer)

```
A woman ran out of a jewelry store with a $50K ring
I had 30 seconds before the cops showed up
I chased her to the corner
Grabbed her wrist
She didn't fight back
She just opened her fist
I took the ring
And held it up so the whole street could see
The owner ran over, out of breath
He kissed the ring
And put it straight back in his window
Then a cop walked up to me
"Sir, empty your pockets"
The whole street went quiet
I turned them inside out
Keys. Phone. Nothing else
"Sorry, sir. Just procedure"
The owner didn't even press charges
"I got my ring back. That's enough"
He tried to hand me $5K
I said, "Keep it, sir"
Everyone clapped

The woman? My sister
The ring I held up? Glass
The real one? Up my sleeve
That's what the 30 seconds were for
Why not just let her run?
Because cops hunt a stolen ring
Nobody hunts a ring that came back
And the $5K?
Why take 5… when you already have 50
The cop checked my pockets
Never my sleeves
That glass ring is still in the owner's window
Priced at $50K
```
