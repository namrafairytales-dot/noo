# The Parking Spot (blind-tested: viewers did not guess the twist)

```
Every morning that week, there was a note on my windshield
"Please move your car before Friday, 5 PM"
It was a free public spot
I'd parked there every single day for 3 months
I ignored it
"Last warning"
I ignored that too
Then a note with $200 taped to it
"PLEASE. Just move it"
I took the $200
And parked there again
Friday, 4:58 PM, I was sitting in my car
A man in a uniform knocked on my window
"Are you the guy? I paid you to move!"
I said, "Two more minutes"
He stormed off around the corner
At 5:00, the door next to my car burst open
Two men in masks jumped into my back seat
Bags in their hands
I didn't even look back
I just drove

That door? The back door of a bank
The men in masks? My brothers
My spot? The only one the bank's cameras couldn't see
That's why I held it for 3 months
And the notes?
The bank's new security guard
His first shift started Friday at 5
Same as ours
He just wanted the spot closest to the door
He paid me $200 to leave
So I left
With $2 million
```
