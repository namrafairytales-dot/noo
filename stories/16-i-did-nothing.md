# He stole my project… so I made him CONFESS on stage 💀 (original revenge, Model 3 GenZ style)

Shape: rage hook, roasts, authority takes the wrong side, an open loop in the middle ("I always hide a little
signature in my work"), then the villain exposes himself out loud. The slide titles spell I-DID-NOTHING.
No violence.

```
My group partner stole my science project, put only his name on it, and entered it in the science fair.
I'd worked on it for 2 weeks.
He worked on it for 2 seconds, long enough to change the name.
"Bro, that's my project."
He shrugged. "Prove it. I submitted it first."
"Because you used my laptop while I was at lunch."
"Sharing is caring."
"Then share the grade."
He laughed. "Relax. I'll thank you in my speech."
"You can't even say 'hypothesis.'"
"Hypo... hypothe... that's not important."
I went to our teacher.
She said, "He submitted it first, so it's his. Sit down, or you get a zero."
Then it got worse.
It won first place.
The principal asked the winner to present it to the whole school.
He walked on stage like a celebrity.
I just smiled, because I always hide a little signature in my work.
After his last slide, I raised my hand.
"Sir, can he read the title of every slide again? Slowly?"
He smirked. "Easy."
"Introduction. Data. Ideas. Design. Notes. Observations. Testing. Hypothesis. Insights. Numbers. Graphs."
The principal frowned and read the first letters out loud.
"I. Did. Nothing."
The whole gym went silent.
Somebody in the back said, "Bro."
He panicked and clicked to the next slide.
Big mistake.
It was a photo of him asleep in class, captioned, "My partner, hard at work."
The principal took the trophy out of his hands and gave it to me.
My best friend leaned over and said, "Bro presented his own confession in 11 slides."
```
