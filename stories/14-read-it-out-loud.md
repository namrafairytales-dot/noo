# My teacher reads your texts OUT LOUD if your phone rings! 💀 (original, Model 3 GenZ style)

```
My new teacher has one rule: if your phone makes a sound in class, she reads your last text out loud.
Monday, a kid's phone buzzed.
She snatched it and read, "Mom: Did you take your vitamins, my little cupcake?"
The whole class burst out laughing.
Tuesday, my best friend's phone dinged.
She read, "Bro, I'm outside."
She smirked. "Outside of what? Passing this class?"
That's when I made a plan.
My mom always says if I get in trouble at school, she'll know before I even get home.
So I texted her one thing: "Please text me during 3rd period."
Wednesday, 3rd period, my phone buzzed.
My teacher smiled like she just won the lottery.
"Rules are rules," she said, and read it to the whole class.
"Hi sweetie, is the teacher who reads kids' texts out loud in the room right now?"
She froze.
Bzzz.
The class started chanting, "Read it! Read it!"
I said, "Ma'am, rules are rules."
She read, "Parents have been calling about her all week."
Her face went red as a tomato.
Bzzz.
"Also, tell her she's parked in my spot again."
She looked around. "Whose spot?"
Bzzz.
She read the last one slowly. "This is the principal. My office. Now."
Then the next line. "And give my son his phone back."
The whole class turned and looked at me.
My mom isn't just my mom.
She's the principal.
My teacher handed my phone back like it was on fire.
Then my best friend leaned over and said, "Bro, she just read her own detention slip out loud."
```

Hook type: authority person + crazy rule (Model 3, hook 2). Twist clues planted early: "new teacher" (she doesn't know
who my mom is) and "she'll know before I even get home". Callback: "Rules are rules". Mic-drop: the best friend.
Safe for YouTube: no violence.
