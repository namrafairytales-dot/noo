# Final Flex 💸 (from the movie 3 Idiots, 2009)

```
10 years getting rich, just to flex on one guy from college
He never memorized anything and still beat me
So I made a bet
"10 years from today, right here, let's see who's bigger"
10 years later, I came back with a big job in America
Big house
Big ego
He didn't show up
So I tracked him down to flex in person
The guy who opened the door had his name
Not his face
Turns out my rival was the gardener's son
He went to college under the rich kid's name, then vanished
We finally found him in the mountains, teaching little kids
A school teacher? I literally laughed

Then I bragged about my big deal
A genius inventor with 400 patents
One signature from him and I'm set
He smiled and told me his real name
The inventor? Him
Bro won the bet without even showing up
```

another name, the teacher in Ladakh and the inventor with 400 patents are all in the film. The last line is mine.

## GenZ funny version (v2)

TITLE: My college rival GHOSTED our 10-year bet! 💀

```
In college, one guy never memorized anything and still beat me.
So I made a bet.
"10 years from today, right here. Let's see who's more successful."
10 years later, I pulled up with a big job in America, a big house, and an even bigger ego.
He didn't show up.
Bro really ghosted a bet.
So I tracked him down to flex in person.
The guy who opened the door had his name.
Not his face.
"Who are you?"
"I'm the real one."
Turns out my rival was the gardener's son.
He went to college under this rich kid's name, handed him the degree, and vanished.
Bro did someone else's degree and still beat me.
We finally found him in the mountains, teaching little kids.
I laughed so hard.
"A school teacher? That's your big success?"
So I flexed harder.
"I'm here to sign a genius inventor. 400 patents. One signature and I'm set for life."
He smiled and told me his real name.
The inventor? Him.
The deal I was bragging about?
I needed it from the guy I just laughed at.
Bro won the bet without even showing up.
```

My jokes (not in the film): "Bro really ghosted a bet", "Bro did someone else's degree and still beat me", the last 3 lines. "I'm the real one" is a paraphrase.
