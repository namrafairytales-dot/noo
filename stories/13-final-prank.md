# Final Prank 🏕️ (from the movie The Parent Trap, 1998)

```
At summer camp, I got into a prank war with my exact lookalike
She was from London
I was from California
It got so bad they sent us to a tiny cabin, alone, together
So we finally talked
Same birthday
I only had a dad
She only had a mom
We each had half of an old torn photo

We put the halves together
My dad and her mom
On their wedding day
We weren't lookalikes
We were twins
Our parents split us up as babies
So we swapped lives
I cut her hair to match mine
She went home to my dad
I went home to her mom
Then my dad got engaged to a 26-year-old
So we got both our parents in the same place
It worked
Lost the prank war
Won a whole sister
```

Source: told by the California twin (Hallie). The camp prank war, the isolation cabin, the same birthday, the
torn wedding photo, the swap, the haircut and the 26-year-old fiancée are all in the film. The last 2 lines are mine.
