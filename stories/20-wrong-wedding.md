# A stranger crashed my cousin's wedding… and gave the BEST speech 💀 (original, new topic and cast)

New topic (wedding crasher), new cast (stranger, aunty, uncle, groom, bride, grandma). Clue planted in line 2
(the calls he keeps ignoring). Twist: he was at the wrong wedding. No violence.

```
A stranger walked into my cousin's wedding, ate 14 plates of food, and nobody knew who he was.
His phone kept buzzing, but he kept ignoring it.
My aunty walked up to him. "Beta, are you from the bride's side or the groom's side?"
He said, "Both."
"Both?"
"I'm on the side of love, aunty." And he grabbed another plate.
My uncle came over. "Who invited you?"
"Bro, real friends don't need invites."
"Then who's the groom?"
He pointed at the stage. "My brother from another mother."
The groom looked at him and said, "I've never seen this man in my life."
The whole hall went quiet.
My uncle smiled like a villain. "If you're such a close friend, give a speech."
He wiped his mouth and grabbed the mic.
"Love is like this biryani."
"Everyone says they've had enough, but they always come back for more."
My aunties gasped.
"And marriage is like the last piece of chicken. You share it, even when you really don't want to."
Grandma started crying.
The bride hugged him. "That's the nicest thing anyone said today."
Even the groom said, "Okay... maybe we did meet once."
Then his phone buzzed again, and he finally picked up. On speaker.
"Bro, where are you?! You're my best man! My wedding started an hour ago in the hall NEXT DOOR!"
He slowly looked at the stage.
"Oh. Wrong couple."
He grabbed one more plate for the road and ran next door.
My grandma watched him leave and said, "Can we keep that one instead?"
```
