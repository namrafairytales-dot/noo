# The Returned Phone

```
A stranger returned my lost phone
The next morning, my bank account was empty
I was at a café
Checked my bank app
Put my phone on the table
And went to the counter for one minute
When I came back, it was gone
I searched every table
Nothing
Ten minutes later, a man walked in
"Is this yours? It was lying outside"
I tried to give him money
He said, "No, no. Just be careful"
I told everyone, "Good people still exist"
I was on Wi-Fi all day
So I didn't notice anything
That night, my phone said "No Service"
I thought it was the network
In the morning, I called the bank
$4,800… gone
Every transfer was approved with a code sent to my number
I said, "My phone had no signal all night"
Then I opened the SIM tray
The SIM inside wasn't mine

The man who brought it back? He's the one who took it
He sat at the next table and watched me type my PIN
Those ten minutes? He swapped my SIM
Every bank code went straight to his phone
If he'd kept my phone, I'd have blocked everything in five minutes
So he gave it back
And told me to be careful
He never wanted my phone
He wanted my number
```
